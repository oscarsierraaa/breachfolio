Contact

Email

The fastest way to reach us is secforgeweb@gmail.com. A real person reads every message. We do not run a support ticket system or a contact form – just a normal inbox.

What to write in about

  • Corrections. Found a factual error, a broken link, or an outdated screenshot? This is the fastest way to get it fixed – see our correction policy.
  • Security reports. Found a vulnerability in Breachfolio itself (not in the third-party tools we write about)? Please include steps to reproduce.
  • Advertising and business. Questions about how the site is funded, or a sponsorship enquiry.
  • Privacy requests. Access, correction, or deletion requests under the policy described in Privacy.
  • Everything else. Feedback, a tip for a future comparison, or just to say hello.

What we do not offer

We do not accept paid placement, sponsored reviews, or guest posts in exchange for links. See Disclosures for the full policy on how the site is funded.
